Our Story & Craft

Good Pizza Goes a Long Way

Rooted on Abbott Road, Lahore, One Way Pizza was created to deliver honest, flavor-packed pizza with bold spices and generous toppings.

Abbott Road, Lahore

Serving The Heart of Lahore

Located on the Ground Floor of Fajar Plaza, Shop 19, opposite Duniya News and near Shimla Pahari Road in Garhi Shahu, One Way Pizza is a destination for late-night foodies, families, and fast-food enthusiasts across central Lahore.

In a city that lives and breathes bold flavors, we believe in keeping things simple and generous: handcrafted crusts, genuine stringy cheese, seasoned chicken, and freshly chopped herbs and garden veggies.

Our Standards

What Sets Our Kitchen Apart

We believe every slice should be piping hot, crisp, and intensely flavorful.

Hand-Tossed Dough

Our dough is mixed fresh daily with the right hydration, creating an airy crumb and a crisp golden outer edge.

Oven-Baked Fresh

Never pre-cooked. Every pizza enters our roaring ovens only after you place your order.

100% Real Mozzarella

We use rich stringy mozzarella that creates that irresistible cheese pull on every single slice.

Late-Night Craving Spot

Operating until 1:30 AM weekdays and 2:00 AM on Saturdays so you never have to sleep hungry.*
